PRIVACY POLICY

How your data is used

Last updated: November 13, 2026. ShiftLife is provided by TAHIR GENCTOG under the LIFEDEVLABS name. For questions, email tahirgenctog@icloud.com.

1. Data stored on your device

Your shift cycles, day templates, goals, projects, routines, session notes, activity records, profile name and selected photo, and app settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them to a server that we operate. Optional iCloud sync (section 6) and plan sharing (section 5) are described separately. Your iOS device backup settings may still apply.

If you export a JSON backup or CSV file in the app, you choose where to share it. Exported files may contain your app data and are subject to the rules of the service where you store them.

2. Optional sign in

You can continue without an account. With Apple sign in, we use the account identifier and, if provided, your name only for the profile link stored on your device. With Google sign in, we use the Google account identifier and, if provided, your name or email address for that local profile link. We do not retain the Google ID token used for profile sign in. The sign in providers process requests under their own privacy policies.

3. Optional calendar connections

If you connect Apple Calendar, we use iOS calendar permission to create a separate ShiftLife calendar in the account you select. We write shifts and, if enabled, handovers about 90 days ahead; planned sleep, family time, rounds, exercise and other timed day blocks are written about 30 days ahead. Each event title, start and end time, and time zone goes to the calendar. Completed sleep and data read from Apple Health are not exported to a calendar. Sync runs one way, from ShiftLife to your calendar. If your selected calendar account syncs with iCloud or another provider, that provider processes those events.

If you connect Google Calendar, we receive your Google account identifier and email to identify the connection, read your calendar list, and manage events only in a separate calendar created by the app. The same 90/30-day planned events, including planned sleep and exercise times, are sent directly to Google with their titles, times, time zones, and management markers and stored on that calendar. Apple Health data is not sent. We do not import events from your other calendars into your ShiftLife plan. The Google refresh token is held in your device Keychain; the access token is held only in memory. We do not use this data for advertising, sale, or other purposes.

When you disconnect normally, we try to remove future events managed by the app. Past events and the created calendar remain. If cleanup cannot finish because of a network or permission issue, future events may remain remotely too; you can manage them in your calendar provider.

4. Optional Apple Health

With your permission, we read sleep, step, and active energy data from Apple Health to display a summary of the past seven days in Analytics. We do not send the Health data we read to our server. We write a completed sleep interval to Apple Health only after you confirm it and select its times. Time and operation records that prevent duplicate writes are kept in a protected file on your device. Deleting app data does not delete records already written to Apple Health; you can manage those in the Health app.

5. Optional plan sharing

With Pro you can choose to share your shift plan with people you invite. If you turn it on, your shifts and hours for the next 42 days (day types and 24-hour blocks) are written to a private CloudKit zone in your own iCloud account, with the content encrypted. Your notes, projects, goals, routines and locations are never shared. The plan opens only to the people you invite in Apple’s sharing sheet, view-only; there is no public link. This data does not go to a server we operate and we cannot access it; Apple’s iCloud service processes it under its own privacy terms. You can stop sharing at any time: the iCloud copy is deleted and invited people can no longer see the plan.

If someone shares a plan with you, the last copy of that plan is kept on this device (in a local file that is excluded from device backups) after you open the invitation. The copy is deleted when you leave the share, when the person sharing stops, when you delete all app data, or when you switch iCloud accounts. Viewing a shared plan is free.

6. Optional iCloud sync

With Pro, if you turn on Settings → iCloud sync, your plans and records in the app (shift cycle and saved shift plans, day templates and categories, exceptions, locations, goals, projects, routines, notes and ideas, activity records, and your shift plan’s sleep and round settings) stay in sync across the devices signed in to the same iCloud account. This data is written to a private CloudKit zone in your own iCloud account; the content is encrypted and record names are hashes that contain no dates or text. Your profile, photo, account details and device preferences are not synced. This data does not go to a server we operate and we cannot access it; Apple’s iCloud service processes it under its own privacy terms. Turning sync off keeps both copies; you can delete your data in iCloud from the same panel. While sync is on, deleting all data or restoring a backup on one device also applies to your other devices. Calendar events written by ShiftLife carry an opaque marker derived from the sync data so that your own devices that connect the calendar while sync is on recognize the same ShiftLife calendar.

7. Notifications, widgets, and purchases

With your permission, reminders are scheduled on your device. Widget data is shared with the app on that same device. You can turn off personal names on the lock screen in app settings.

Pro purchases are processed by Apple's StoreKit system. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks verified purchase entitlements and keeps a local status record. You manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

8. Sharing, support, and this website

The app has no ads, third party analytics SDK, or tracking data sharing. We send data to Apple or Google only to run the connections you choose, the optional plan sharing and iCloud sync. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond to your support request. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may process access logs under its own privacy statement.

9. Deletion and requests

Use Settings → Data & Backup → Delete all data or Settings → Account → Delete account and data in the app. You can disconnect Google Calendar in Settings and separately remove access in your Google account. Use the relevant app to manage records held by Apple Health or your calendar provider. If you share your plan, both deletion flows also end the share and delete the iCloud copy. If iCloud sync is on, Delete all data also applies to your other devices, and Delete account and data also deletes your sync data in iCloud; to leave a plan someone shared with you, use Settings → Shared plans → Leave share. Files that you exported earlier must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

For privacy questions, access, or deletion requests, email tahirgenctog@icloud.com. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations.

10. Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The current date appears at the top of this page.
