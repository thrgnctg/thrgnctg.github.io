LIFE PLANNING AROUND SHIFT CYCLES

Your shifts change.
Keep your own rhythm.

ShiftLife is an iPhone app that helps you build a repeating shift cycle and see work, rest, routines, and goals in one plan, even when your schedule does not fit a seven day week.

01

Build your cycle

Set shift windows and day templates. Your plan for today and upcoming days is calculated from that cycle.

02

Make room for life

Connect routines, projects, and goals to your plan. Review completed work and your rhythm in Analytics.

03

Connect if you choose

With permission, write shifts, optional handovers, and timed plan blocks such as sleep, family time and rounds to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar, and view seven day sleep, step, and active energy summaries from Apple Health.

You stay in control

You can use ShiftLife without an account. Plans are stored on your device in this release; automatic ShiftLife sync across devices is not available. Calendar and Health connections are optional. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
